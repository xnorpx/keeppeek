These are recorded demonstrations, not a live preview of the current build. Check the published manifest for the source commit, generation timestamp, content hashes, and scenario metadata for every asset.

Set up a camera with catalog context

Discover a model, review embedded CCTV Database catalog facts, open the outbound catalog link for deeper research, search a quiet camera manually, and review editable stream suggestions before the first configuration write.

Manage credentials, camera grants, and sessions

Create a named User credential in Settings > Access, scope its access to selected groups and cameras, and revoke an active session.

Operate PTZ, motion detection, and Talk

Drive continuous PTZ with explicit Stop PTZ, recall a saved preset, toggle camera motion detection with verified read-back, and inspect the Administrator Talk speaker boundary on /camera.

Add nine cameras and watch the live wall

Start from an empty KeepPeek server, configure nine independently paced RTSP sources, watch all nine feeds join the shared WebRTC live wall, and inspect one stream’s diagnostics.

Review what just happened

Focus a live camera on Peek, then use History to open its timeline in Keep.

Inspect details with digital zoom and pan

Zoom a live or recorded viewport from 1.0x to 4.0x, pan within picture bounds by drag and keyboard, and reset to 1.0x without moving physical PTZ hardware.

Review recorded footage and export evidence in Keep

Inspect recorded spans and gaps on the UTC timeline, browse Stories & Calendar and Swimlanes, create an evidence export with burn-in timestamps and SHA-256 verification, and inspect bounded Timelapse review and silent MP4 export.

Filter events, inspect detail evidence, and review Activity

Build a structured boolean filter in Events, inspect a matched card’s canonical thumbnail, bounding box, and bookmark actions, then open Activity to inspect bucket charts, weekday×hour heatmaps, and CSV/JSON export.

Configure, issue, preview, and revoke a guest review link

Enable canonical HTTPS guest sharing policy, issue a one-time bounded link from a ready export, preview the isolated /share guest surface, and revoke the grant with an audit reason.

Triage fleet health and diagnose a camera

Inspect fleet verdicts and evidence dimensions on /system-health, drill into an offline camera, and apply Switch to TCP on a UDP-degraded camera.

Manage camera templates and bulk fleet changes

Inspect inherited versus overridden camera fields on /cameras, review templates preserving {secret:...} references, preview a server-owned bulk diff, and apply it with per-camera results.

Export, stage, and validate a configuration ZIP

Export the two-file config.toml + secrets.toml ZIP archive, stage a verified upload pending explicit server restart, and observe rejection handling for an invalid archive.

Preview recording deletion and reconcile catalog drift

Preview bounded whole-recording deletion with typed DELETE N confirmation and durable ledger tracking, then inspect and reconcile catalog drift on /recordings/maintenance.

Configure notification rules, mobile push, and MQTT 5

Inspect notification rules, Inbox, and History, test bounded Keeppeek Service mobile push signaling, and inspect and test the MQTT 5 forwarder.

Configure the direct Home Assistant Lovelace card

Load camera sources in the Home Assistant visual card editor, switch grid columns and single-camera layout, and focus and unfocus a live tile.

Inspect appearance, live logs, diagnostics, and bug reports
